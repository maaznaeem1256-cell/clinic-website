Our Story

A legacy of compassion, rooted in community.

The Founding Story

Parveen Aslam Memorial Clinic was established in 2023 by Adil Waheed in loving memory of his mother, Parveen Aslam. What began as a deeply personal tribute has grown into a trusted healthcare destination for Chokrian and the surrounding villages of Tehsil Shahkot.

We recognized the pressing need for accessible, modern healthcare facilities in our local community. Our clinic stands as a testament to a mother's caring spirit and a son's dedication to improving the lives of his people.

Our Mission

To serve the local community with quality, accessible primary and women's healthcare, delivered with dignity and respect.

A Tribute in Care

This clinic embodies the enduring legacy of Parveen Aslam. Her life was defined by selflessness and a profound empathy for others. By providing vital medical care to those who need it most, we honor her memory every single day. The clinic is not just a building; it is the continuation of her compassion, ensuring that her care for humanity lives on.
